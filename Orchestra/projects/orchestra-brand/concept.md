# Orchestration brand: concept

## Concept


## Confirmed from Figma (Oct 30)

- Orchestration Blue: **#0538FF**, sampled from the Brand > Thumbnail background.
- Logo: white ∫ inside a thin white circle, transparent background (Brand > Orchestration Logo).
- Wordmark: Orchestration Type Mark, outlined vector.
- Imagery: Violin ASCII, built from rawpixel image 3282518 (check its license: CC0 or paid).

## Brand decisions (Oct 30)

- Tagline: **Every part, in concert.** (was "Many hands. One score." until Nov 2: "hands" read badly)
- Mark: ∫ centred in the ring (nudged +0.5 u). 180° rotational symmetry, terminal axis 33.4°, spine 16 u, height:ring 184:300.
- Clear space 2x (x = terminal Ø 22 u). Min size 24 px with ring, 16 px for the ∫ alone.
- Brand blue mark is for light backgrounds only (2.8:1 on Ink).
- Signal doubles as warning/needs-you; text on Signal is Ink. Mist (blue/200) = agent activity, never text on white.
- Radius sharp: 0 containers, 2 controls, full for pills/toggles. Modes: Light (default) + Dark.
- Icons: 48 Remix icons carried over from Pitcrew.
- Tokens: `prereqs/tokens.json` (exported from Figma).

## Brand sync (Nov 2)

- Tried Montserrat for body/UI, reverted the same day: Instrument Sans stays.
- App icon: round Blue disc, ring and ∫ thickened from Mark Bold for small sizes (Brand > Logo/App icon round).
- Dark mode unchanged: the site's navy dark mode is not adopted.
- Added to Foundations > Motion, states & additions: selected-state type, motion curves and timings, Navy / Ink 3 / box blue, space/32, grain, agent and phase icons, site patterns. Site-only items marked.
