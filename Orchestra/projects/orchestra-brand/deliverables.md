# Deliverables

Picked in Orchestra on 2026-11-07. The Orchestrator builds the foundations first, once each, then briefs one Musician per deliverable.

## Foundations, built first

1. **Product Brief**: prereqs/product-brief.md
2. **Brand Spec**: prereqs/brand-spec.md and prereqs/tokens.json
3. **Logo Concepts**: prereqs/logo-concepts.md
4. **Logo Builder**: Logo frames in Figma, final files in prereqs/logo/
5. **Voice Guide**: prereqs/voice-guide.md
6. **Deck Outline**: prereqs/deck-outline.md

## Deliverables

- **Brand Kit**: The brand's visual source of truth in Figma: color, type, logo, components and slide masters. Needs Brand Spec, Logo Builder.
- **Logo**: The logo, from written directions to final vector files in every format the brand needs. Needs Product Brief.
- **Motion**: Motion made in Figma, starting with logo animations (reveal, loops, transitions). Needs Brand Spec, Logo Builder.
- **Slide Deck**: A presentation in Figma built on the brand kit's slide masters: pitch, product, or client deck. Needs Product Brief, Brand Spec, Voice Guide, Deck Outline.
